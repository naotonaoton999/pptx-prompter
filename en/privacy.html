Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

PPTX Prompter does not collect or transmit your personal information or presentation content to the developer's servers. Appearance preferences such as font size, line spacing and margins are saved locally on your Mac.

Information processed locally

The .pptx files you select and the presentation information obtained from Microsoft PowerPoint are processed only on your Mac to provide the app's features. This information is not transmitted to the developer's servers or to third-party servers.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

PPTX Prompter does not use advertising, user tracking or external analytics services.

Microsoft PowerPoint integration

PPTX Prompter uses macOS Automation permission to access Microsoft PowerPoint. This access is limited to reading slideshow status, the active presentation name and the current slide number.

PPTX Prompter does not use this permission to advance slides, edit presentations or speaker notes, save files or otherwise control PowerPoint.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact information on our Support page.

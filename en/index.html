PPTX Prompter

PPTX Prompter follows your Microsoft PowerPoint slideshow and displays the current slide's speaker notes in large, easy-to-read text on your Mac.

Privacy Policy Support

Features

  • Automatically follows the current slide in a PowerPoint slideshow
  • Reads speaker notes locally from the .pptx file you select
  • Displays notes in large text on a black background
  • Adjustable font size, line spacing and margins
  • Version 1.1 and later: Adjust font size while the prompter is visible with Control–Option–Command–Up/Down (4 pt per press)
  • Supports bold, underlined and italic formatting
  • Scroll through long notes with a mouse wheel or trackpad
  • Choose the prompter display when using multiple monitors
  • Press Command–Option–P to temporarily hide or restore the prompter

Getting started

Install Microsoft PowerPoint for Mac separately. Open a presentation with speaker notes in PowerPoint, select the same .pptx file in PPTX Prompter, allow macOS Automation access when prompted, and start the slideshow.

Privacy

PPTX Prompter processes the presentation you select locally on your Mac. Your presentation and speaker notes are not transmitted to external servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
